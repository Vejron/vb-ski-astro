# Sound effects

Recorded sounds in this folder are processed (trimmed, mono, pitched and layered) by
`tools/sfx/build_sfx.py` from these CC0 (public domain) sources:

- The Free Firearm Sound Library, "Prepared SFX Library" (bart) — https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library — rifle, revolver, sniper, mg, cannon, boom
- Impacts/gunshots (Eldritch Grim) — https://opengameart.org/content/impactsgunsfx — downloaded, not used in the current set
- synthesized explosion (qubodup) — https://opengameart.org/content/synthesized-explosion — boom
- soundpack 03 (crazyduckgames) — https://opengameart.org/content/soundpack-03 — blast

All four are CC0 1.0; no attribution is required, it is given as a courtesy.
